Comboios MCP

An unofficial connector for Portuguese train information, for Claude and other MCP clients.

Not affiliated with CP – Comboios de Portugal. It reads the same public data cp.pt shows (timetables, fares, live departures, alerts). Data may be incomplete or delayed; check cp.pt before you travel.

Add it to Claude

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector.
  2. Paste this URL and save. No sign-in is needed.
https://YOUR-DOMAIN/mcp

Then ask, for example: “Próximos comboios de Lisboa Oriente para o Porto amanhã de manhã” or “Is the 17:40 Alfa Pendular from Campanhã on time?”

What it can do

  • Find stations and their facilities.
  • Search journeys with times, changes and fares (1st and 2nd class).
  • Live departures and arrivals for a station, and the live position and delay of a train.
  • Current service alerts and strikes.
  • Buy tickets on a payment page here, choosing your seat on trains with seat reservation (card, MB WAY or PayPal, issued and charged by CP). For a return trip, each direction is a separate ticket.

Privacy

Searching, timetables, live information and alerts need no personal data. Requests are passed to CP's public website services; the server keeps short-lived caches of timetable data and standard request logs.

If you buy a ticket on the payment page, the passenger name(s), email and optional phone and ID document you enter are sent to CP to issue the ticket. They are not stored or logged by this service. Card, MB WAY and PayPal details are entered in Checkout.com's form, which CP uses, and never reach this service. The ticket is issued and charged by CP; for refunds or problems, contact CP with your purchase reference.
